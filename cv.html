Profile

Computational scientist working on uncertainty quantification (UQ) and machine-learning surrogate models for multiphysics, multiscale simulation on high-performance computing systems. PhD research at the Technical University of Munich (TUM) and Max Planck Institute for Plasma Physics (IPP) addressed UQ for coupled plasma turbulent transport. Postdoctoral work at Bangor University focused on tritium transport in lithium-ceramic breeder materials. Earlier industrial experience includes production C++ scientific software at Siemens Corporate Technology.
