Yehor Yudin

Computational scientist and research software engineer working on uncertainty quantification, machine-learning surrogates, and reliable software for computational fusion science.

My work spans high-performance plasma simulations and multiphysics modelling of tritium transport in fusion materials. I build open-source tools that help researchers quantify uncertainty, validate models, and make computational workflows more reproducible.

Previously a Research Officer at Bangor University, a doctoral researcher at the Max Planck Institute for Plasma Physics, and a scientific software engineer at Siemens. I am interested in postdoctoral research and research software engineering opportunities in computational fusion science.
