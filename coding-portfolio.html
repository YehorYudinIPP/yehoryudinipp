Coding Portfolio

Open-source scientific software and research engineering across uncertainty quantification, multiscale simulation, and computational fusion science.

Featured repositories

Selected projects and contributions spanning UQ workflows, surrogate modelling, and ensemble-data visualisation.

  • FESTIM-NIUQ: Python package for non-intrusive UQ of FESTIM hydrogen and tritium-transport simulations using EasyVVUQ; developed within the TRIMAX/LIBRTI research programme.
  • OpenMC-NIUQ: non-intrusive UQ workflows for OpenMC neutronics models.
  • EasySurrogate: contributions to the VECMA/SEAVEA surrogate-modelling toolkit, including Gaussian-process models and EasyVVUQ integration.
  • FUMEplot: open-source visualisation of uncertainty in ensemble modelling outputs; co-authored a paper describing the prototype.
  • TRIMAX: uncertainty-aware multiphysics modelling and experimental validation for lithium-based fusion breeder materials, in collaboration with Bangor University, UKAEA, and the University of Birmingham HF-ADNeF facility.
  • Homecoming: Python-based agent modelling of refugee return, a UNHCR-commissioned project with Brunel University.

Focus areas

  • Non-intrusive uncertainty quantification, sensitivity analysis, and surrogate modelling
  • Scientific Python, C++, HPC, and multiphysics simulation workflows
  • Research software engineering: testing, CI, APIs, documentation, and open-source release

Tools and methods

Python, C++, C, Fortran, MPI, OpenMP, CUDA, OpenACC, SLURM, PyTorch, scikit-learn, NumPy, SciPy, EasyVVUQ, FESTIM, OpenMC, MOOSE, Gaussian-process regression, polynomial chaos, and Bayesian calibration.
